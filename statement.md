# Hospital Management System

## 1. Problem Statement

Managing hospital information manually can be time-consuming and may lead to errors in maintaining patient, doctor, and appointment records. The **Hospital Management System** is developed to provide a simple computerized solution for organizing and managing hospital-related information efficiently. It helps reduce manual work and makes accessing and updating records easier.

## 2. Scope of the Project

The scope of this project is to provide a basic system for managing important hospital activities and records. The system can be extended to include patient management, doctor management, appointment scheduling, billing, medical records, and user authentication. It can be further improved with a database and advanced reporting features.

## 3. Target Users

The main target users of the Hospital Management System are:

* **Hospital Administrators** – Manage hospital information and system records.
* **Receptionists** – Manage patient details and appointments.
* **Doctors** – Access relevant patient and appointment information.
* **Hospital Staff** – Maintain and update hospital-related records.

## 4. High-Level Features

The major features of the system include:

* Patient information management
* Doctor information management
* Appointment management
* Adding, viewing, updating, and deleting records
* Organized hospital data management
* User-friendly interface
* Basic validation and error handling
* Easy access to hospital records
